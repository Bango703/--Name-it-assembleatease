# Owner Information Architecture

Status: target contract

```text
Owner Overview -> what needs attention?
Bookings -> what exists?
Booking Detail -> what happened?
Dispatch -> who is assigned or needs assignment?
Finance Overview -> are we financially healthy?
Transactions -> what money moved?
Payouts -> what do we owe Easers?
Growth -> where is business coming from?
System -> is the platform healthy?
```

A booking detail view should show one primary next action, not raw internal state. Payment, evidence, assignment, payout, messages, audit, and timeline remain separate tabs or sections.
