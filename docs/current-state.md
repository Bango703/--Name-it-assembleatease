## Known limitations (verified)
- Four different "same area" rules: automatic-dispatch ZIPs, service markets, `isTexasZip` (defined twice) and `_easer-coverage.js`; browser copies in `booking-source-of-truth.js`, `book.html` and `aae-location.js` (Austin only). Details: `docs/decisions/2026-11-13-dispatch-contract-audit.md`.
- Dallas-Fort Worth bookings wait for manual assignment although that market has active Easers. Houston (ZIPs 770-775, Greater Houston) joined automatic dispatch on 2026-11-18 on the owner's authorization (`HOUSTON_MARKET_ZIP_PREFIXES` in `api/_source-of-truth.js`, own `houston_metro` service-call zone labelled "Greater Houston").
- ~~`isSameServiceMarket` fails open when either ZIP maps to no market.~~ Fixed on `fix/dispatch-service-areas`: it fails closed and no longer decides offers (`serviceAreaCheck` does).
- Custom-quote price requests are owner-triggered; no automatic asking.
- No time-overlap check between jobs (daily cap only); travel distance is straight-line between ZIP points, not drive time; no address validation at booking.
- Not built: net-30 partner invoicing, per-item partner price sheets, native Easer price-request screen, Checkr background checks.
- Easer identity follow-up (2026-11-20, `api/cron/identity-followup.js`, daily 15:17 UTC): applicants with a signed agreement and no verified ID get a fresh 72-hour Stripe Identity link on days 1, 3 and 7 after their last ID activity (email, plus text with consent); the owner is emailed after the third. Stripe sends no webhook when an applicant quits mid-check (session `requires_input`, last_error `abandoned`), so the profile keeps saying `pending`; the cron reads Stripe directly. It never marks anyone verified; a Stripe-verified session the profile does not reflect is reported to the owner.
