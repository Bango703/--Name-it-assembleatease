# Customer Information Architecture

Status: target contract

Customer surfaces answer:

- What do I have booked?
- Who is coming?
- When?
- What is happening now?
- Do I owe anything?
- Do I have a message?

Customer booking detail contains overview, timeline, professional, messages, photos, payment, receipt, reschedule, cancel, support, and review. It must not expose internal payment states, owner operations, Easer administration, or provider debugging.
