Where Texans buy furniture — and who builds it.

An honest, unsponsored guide to the furniture stores Texans actually shop across Austin, Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, and San Antonio — plus the easy part everyone forgets: getting it assembled the day it lands in your living room.

Common questions

Furniture & assembly, answered.

Do Texas furniture stores assemble the furniture you buy?

Some offer delivery with basic setup for an extra fee, but flat-pack retailers like IKEA generally don’t include assembly at all. Most Texans hire a dedicated assembly service so the bed, wardrobe, desk, or sofa is built correctly the day it arrives.

Who assembles IKEA furniture in Texas?

AssembleAtEase assembles IKEA and other flat-pack furniture across Austin and the wider Texas metros. You see a clear starting price before you book and pay after the work is done right.

Do you assemble furniture bought online, like Wayfair or Amazon?

Yes. It doesn’t matter where you bought it — in store or online. If it comes in a box, we build it, mount it if needed, and clear away the packaging.
