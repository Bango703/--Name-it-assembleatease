# White-Hat SEO & Authority Plan

The single reference for how AssembleAtEase earns organic ranking honestly. Ordered by
**priority and dependency**, not by calendar. No dates, no black-hat tactics — every
asset must earn real value.

---

## Diagnosis (why rankings are stuck)

Technical SEO passes and the site already has more pages than it needs. The two real
limiters are:

1. **Authority** — almost no other sites link to us (referring domains are near zero).
2. **Local proof** — too few reviews and real local signals for Google to trust us in
   a given market.

Therefore the plan spends its effort on **links, citations, and reviews** — not on more
pages. Adding thin/duplicate pages makes the domain look weaker, not stronger.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)

- **No black-hat.** No doorway pages, no cloaking, no hidden pages, no rigged
  "directories," no bought links, no duplicate/city-swapped pages. These risk a Google
  penalty and legal exposure for near-zero upside.
- **Every page earns real value.** A page ships only if a real customer is genuinely
  helped by it. "For SEO" is never a sufficient reason.
- **Completion before expansion.** Strengthen existing core pages before creating any
  new ones. New city pages stay frozen until existing pages earn results.
- **Honesty over tricks.** We can make our strengths obvious; we never pretend to be a
  neutral third party or misrepresent competitors.

---

## Workstream 1 — Foundation & one source of truth (do first; everything depends on it)

- **Lock the NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) to ONE exact format and use it identically
  everywhere online. Inconsistent NAP quietly suppresses local ranking.
- **Keep the Google Business Profile fully accurate** — correct primary category, real
  services, real photos, correct service areas, and a booking link.
- **Measure with a Search Console scorecard** — clicks, impressions, CTR, average
  position, number of referring domains, number of reviews. This proves what works.

*Owner seats: CTO (source of truth), Data (measurement).*

## Workstream 2 — Local citations (foundational, low-effort)

Get listed, with **identical NAP**, in the core local sources:

- Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Nextdoor, Facebook, Better Business Bureau,
  the local Chamber of Commerce, plus a short list of reputable home-services directories.

These are table stakes: they confirm the business exists and is consistent. They are not
the needle-mover, but the needle-mover (Workstream 3) works better once these are in place.

*Owner seat: SEO / Ops (the sign-ups are an owner action).*

## Workstream 3 — Real backlinks via partnerships (the actual needle-mover)

The highest-value work. Earn a small number of **genuine editorial/partner links** from
businesses whose customers need assembly:

- **Targets:** apartment & property managers, realtors, movers, furniture/retail stores,
  interior designers, chambers of commerce, suppliers, and completed-job partners.
- **Tactics:** a "recommended assembly partner" link on their site, move-in referral
  pages, supplier/vendor listings, a local event sponsorship, and "trusted by [partner]"
  relationships built from real completed work.
- **Supporting asset:** a clean `/partners` page that makes it effortless for a partner
  to understand and link to us, plus a simple outreach tracker and honest outreach copy.
- **Discipline:** quality over quantity. A handful of real, relevant links outperform
  hundreds of directory links. Never buy links or create fake profiles.

This is where the first real customers *and* the first real authority come from at the
same time — partner distribution is the through-line.

*Owner seats: Head of Partnerships (relationships), SEO (link hygiene).*

## Workstream 4 — Reviews & real proof (mostly automated already)

Reviews are a top local ranking factor. The automated review engine already requests a
review from every eligible completed customer (spaced, stops once they review, suppressed
during disputes) and offers the optional Google-review link.

- **Drive volume** by completing jobs and letting the engine run.
- **Respond to every review** (positive and negative) — it signals an active, trusted
  business.
- **Add real job photos/reviews only where real work happened** — never fabricated.

*Owner seats: Customer Success, Trust & Safety.*

## Workstream 5 — On-page value (supports, does not replace, authority)

- **One honest decision/comparison page** — e.g. "how to choose a furniture-assembly pro"
  — genuinely useful, fair, and clearly ours. It must be **unique**, not a reworded copy
  of an existing service or city page, or it should not ship.
- **Sharper titles & meta descriptions** on the priority pages so they earn clicks as
  rankings improve.
- **Internal linking** — point the comparison page and existing blog posts at the money
  (booking) pages.
- **No new city/service pages** until existing pages earn results.

*Owner seats: SEO, Brand & Design-System (no duplication, on-brand).*

---

## Priority order (what matters most, in sequence)

1. **Foundation** — NAP + GBP accuracy + scorecard (Workstream 1).
2. **Reviews** — already automated; keep completing jobs (Workstream 4).
3. **Citations** — the table-stakes listings (Workstream 2).
4. **Partner links** — the real lever; start as soon as the foundation is set and treat
   it as continuous (Workstream 3).
5. **On-page** — the one honest comparison page + title/meta + internal links
   (Workstream 5).
6. **New pages** — paused.

## Success signals (watch these, not a clock)

- Referring domains rising from near-zero toward a healthy handful of real, relevant sites.
- Average position climbing off the bottom of the results.
- Click-through rate rising as titles improve and rankings climb.
- Review count growing steadily with responses on all.
- A rising share of bookings attributed to organic search.

Authority compounds gradually — judge progress by these signals moving in the right
direction, not by any fixed deadline.

---

## What this plan explicitly rejects

- Hidden pages, doorway pages, cloaking, or rigged "directories."
- Listing competitors under a false pretense of neutrality.
- Buying links or creating duplicate/city-swapped pages.
- Publishing any page whose only purpose is SEO.
