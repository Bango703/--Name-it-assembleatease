# Product Capability Checklist

Status: inventory

Implemented or substantially present:

- customer booking and tracking
- Easer applications, readiness, offers, assignments, and execution
- owner operations and finance surfaces
- Stripe authorization, capture, refund, dispute, and Connect payout paths
- Telnyx SMS, voice, and call control
- Resend email and delivery webhooks
- evidence uploads and review
- support cases and activity timelines
- scheduled jobs and reconciliation workers
- audit and operational event records

Partial or target work:

- centralized outbox and queue model
- normalized conversation and delivery model
- explicit owner permission roles
- unified policy decision engine
- dedicated reconciliation center
- staging and full three-role E2E environment
- full architecture guard CI
- dedicated domain-oriented folder structure

This checklist describes capability status, not launch readiness.
