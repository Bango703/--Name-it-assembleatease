IKEA MALM Dresser Built and Anchored From $109

Skip the drawer-by-drawer afternoon: what MALM assembly involves, why it is anchored to the wall, how to prep and what it costs.

By AssembleAtEase Editorial Team · Updated Oct 22, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

A MALM dresser is a flat-pack piece with many drawer parts, and it is fixed to the wall with the anti-tip restraint in the box. Assembly of a dresser with up to six drawers is $109, and an eight drawer or double dresser is $149.

MALM is one of IKEA's most common dressers, sold in three, four, six and eight drawer versions. It goes together with cam locks, dowels and drawer runners, and most of the time goes into the drawers rather than the frame.

IKEA includes a wall restraint with every MALM and its instructions say to attach it. This guide covers the build, the anchoring and how to get the room ready.

What is in the boxes

A MALM arrives as one or two flat boxes with panels, bags of hardware sorted by letter, drawer runners and the wall restraint. Check every panel for chips or cracks before assembly starts, and count the hardware against the list in the instructions.

If a part is missing or damaged, IKEA sends replacement parts. Note the part number from the instructions so the request is quick.

  • Check panels for chips, cracks or crushed corners
  • Match the hardware bags against the parts list
  • Keep the instruction booklet with its part numbers
  • Find the wall restraint and keep it with the hardware

How the build goes

The frame goes together first: sides, top and base joined with cam locks and dowels, then the thin back panel nailed in place, which squares the frame. If the back panel goes on out of square, the drawers rub, so this step is checked carefully.

The drawers take most of the time. Each one has a front, sides, a back and a bottom panel, plus runners fixed inside the frame. An eight drawer MALM is mostly a drawer project.

Anchoring it to the wall

A dresser can tip forward when several drawers are pulled open, especially if a child climbs it. The MALM restraint fixes the top of the dresser to the wall so it cannot fall forward.

The restraint is in the box, but the screws and wall plugs are not, because walls differ. Drywall over a wooden stud, drywall without a stud and masonry each need a different fixing. Pick up the right ones for your wall before the visit; the pro brings the drill and finds the stud.

  • Drywall with a stud behind it: wood screws into the stud
  • Drywall without a stud: hollow-wall anchors rated for the load
  • Brick or concrete: masonry plugs and screws
  • Rentals: check with the landlord before drilling

Prepare the room

Clear a work area beside the spot where the dresser will stand, and decide its exact position against the wall, because a finished MALM is heavy to move.

  • Clear the floor where the dresser goes and a work area beside it
  • Decide the final position against the wall
  • Have the wall screws or plugs for your wall type
  • Empty and move the old dresser if it is being replaced

What it costs

Assembly of a dresser with up to six drawers is $109, which covers the three, four and six drawer MALM. An eight drawer MALM or another double dresser is $149, and a matching nightstand is $79.

If an old dresser is leaving, disposal is $49 per piece, and moving a finished piece to another room is $35 per piece.

  • Dresser, up to 6 drawers: $109
  • Dresser, 7 or more drawers or double: $149
  • Nightstand: $79
  • Furniture disposal: $49 per piece
  • Move to another room: $35 per piece

Doing the whole bedroom in one visit

A MALM rarely arrives alone. The matching bed frame, nightstands and a wardrobe usually come in the same delivery, and one visit for all of them means one booking and one afternoon of noise instead of several.

The Bedroom Ready bundle books the bed, the dresser and a nightstand together at the same item prices, with nothing added for the package. A queen bed frame is $119, a dresser with up to six drawers is $109 and a nightstand is $79, and you can add anything else in the room to the same visit.

After assembly

Each drawer should open and close smoothly and sit level with its neighbours. Small gaps can be evened out at the runners; a drawer that rubs usually points to a back panel that went on out of square.

Keep the leftover hardware and the instructions. They make it easy to tighten things later or take the dresser apart for a move.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to anchor a MALM dresser?

IKEA includes a wall restraint with every MALM and its instructions say to attach it. It keeps the dresser from tipping forward.

Why are wall screws not included?

Walls differ, so IKEA leaves the screws and plugs to you. Buy wood screws for a stud, anchors for hollow drywall or masonry plugs for brick.

How much does MALM dresser assembly cost?

A dresser with up to six drawers is $109, and an eight drawer or double dresser is $149, before tax.

What if a part is missing?

IKEA sends replacement parts. Note the part number from the instructions when you request it.

Can the old dresser be taken away?

Yes. Furniture disposal is $49 per piece and is booked with the assembly.
