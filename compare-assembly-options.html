Five questions to ask before you book.

Whoever you hire — us or someone else — these answers tell you almost everything about how the job will go. If a service dodges any of them, that’s your answer.

1

Is the price clear before I book?

A trustworthy service gives you a real starting price up front. “We’ll figure it out when we get there” is how small jobs turn into big invoices.

2

Are the pros identity-verified?

Someone is coming into your home. You should know they were identity-checked and reviewed — not just whoever claimed the job first.

3

When do I pay?

The safest arrangement is paying after the work is done right. Be cautious with large upfront deposits before anyone lifts a screwdriver.

4

What if something goes wrong?

Ask how they handle a wobbly build, a wrong part, or accidental damage. A real service has an answer before you ask.

5

Can I see real reviews?

Verified reviews from completed jobs beat a vague “5 stars” with no source. No reviews anywhere is a reason to keep looking.

+

Is scheduling realistic?

Same-day sounds great — ask what’s actually available for your date and item, and whether they confirm quickly or leave you waiting.
