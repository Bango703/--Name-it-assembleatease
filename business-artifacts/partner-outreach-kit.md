# Partner Outreach Kit — Move-In / Referral Channel

**Goal:** land the first 25 jobs through people who already stand next to customers at the exact moment they need assembly — not ads. One relationship (a property manager, a realtor, a furniture store) can send more jobs than 50 blog posts. Work **one** channel for 30 days before adding a second.

**Why this channel fits us:** our Room-Ready Bundles and Move-In Pass are built for people who just bought furniture and have no tools. That is precisely the moment a mover, realtor, or apartment manager is standing in the room.

**Tracking:** every partner link and the QR card carry `utm_source=partner`, so completed bookings from this channel show up attributed. The QR points to the pre-filled move-in bundle:
`https://www.assembleatease.com/book?bundle=move-in-ready&utm_source=partner`

---

## 1. Who to approach (in priority order)

| Partner | Why they refer | Where the customer is |
|---|---|---|
| **Apartment / property managers, leasing offices** | New tenant every lease; they want move-in to feel easy and reduce "help me" requests | Signing a lease, getting keys |
| **Realtors / closing agents** | A closing gift that actually gets used; makes them look thoughtful | Just closed, empty house |
| **Local moving companies** | Movers carry boxes but don't assemble; we're the natural hand-off, not a competitor | Boxes just dropped, nothing built |
| **Furniture / fitness / mattress stores** | Customers ask "can someone put this together?" at the register | Buying the item |
| **Interior designers / home stagers** | They need pieces assembled on a deadline, reliably | Staging / install day |

Start with the top two. They have the most new-home moments and the least effort to say yes.

## 2. The 20-second pitch (in person / phone)

> "Hi — I run AssembleAtEase, a local assembly and setup service. When your [tenants / clients] move in, they've usually got furniture, a TV, maybe a treadmill — and no tools and no time. We handle all of it, upfront flat price, and their card isn't charged until the job's done right. I'd love to be the person you point them to. It makes you look good and costs you nothing. Can I leave a few cards at your desk?"

Then hand them the counter-card (QR). That's the whole ask.

## 4. What we say that competitors can't

Lead with these three every time — they're our real wedge:

1. **Upfront flat price — no bidding war.** (vs Thumbtack/Angi lead-gen and haggling.)
2. **Pay after it's done — the card is held, not charged until completion.** (vs pay-a-stranger-upfront.)
3. **Reviewed local pros + we come back free if it's not right.** (vs faceless big-box assembly.)

Never position as "cheap" — we're professional, organized, and trustworthy inside the home.

## 5. The ask (make it a yes)

- Leave **5–10 counter-cards** (the QR card) at their desk.
- Offer to add our booking link to their move-in packet / closing folder / email footer.
- Optional partner thank-you: offer a small AssembleCash credit or a per-completed-referral thank-you **only after** the channel proves out — don't lead with money.

## 6. Measure it (30-day check)

- Cards handed out and to whom (keep a simple list).
- Bookings with `utm_source=partner` in the owner dashboard.
- Completed jobs from the channel → which partner drove them → double down on that one.

The number that matters is **completed jobs from a partner**, not cards printed. If one partner drives 3 jobs in 30 days, that's your channel — go deeper there before adding a second.

## 7. Starter target list — Austin (start here this week)

Researched, real, local. **Do not blast.** Send 1:1 and personalized from your real address, or walk in. Find the specific contact on the company site or LinkedIn; if unsure, call and ask *"who handles resident/customer partnerships?"* Aim for **10 contacts a week**.

**Movers — the easiest yes** (they carry boxes but don't assemble; you're the hand-off, not a competitor). Use **Template C**.
- Einstein Moving Company — large, very well-known Austin local
- The Moving Kings ATX
- ATX Movers
- Busy Bee Moving & Storage
- Harder's Movers
- Austin Pro Moving (family-owned, 20+ yrs)
- Infinity Moving
→ Reach the owner or dispatcher; offer reciprocal referrals (you'll send moving leads their way too).

**Apartment communities / property managers** — recurring move-in volume, one relationship = many residents. Use **Template A**; for leasing offices, walking in with cards beats email.
- Big multifamily: drive your service-area ZIPs and note every leasing office — each large complex has one and turns over leases constantly. Also pull communities from apartments.com for your ZIPs.
- Management firms: Rollingwood Management, PMI Austin, Keyrenter Austin, Eanes Properties, HomeRiver Group, Stream Realty (more commercial).
→ Best contact: Community Manager / Resident Experience / Leasing Manager.

**Realtors** — the "closing gift" angle. Use **Template B**.
- Warmest path first: any agent you or a friend already know.
- Then high-volume buyer's agents/teams (find via Zillow / Realtor.com "top Austin agents"). Target buyer's agents — their clients are the ones moving into empty homes.

**Find 20 real prospects in 30 minutes:** Google Maps → search *"apartments"*, *"moving company"*, *"real estate agent"* in each of your service ZIPs → grab name, phone, website → movers/realtors get a 1:1 email or DM; apartments get a walk-in or leasing-office call → log every one in the tracker below.
