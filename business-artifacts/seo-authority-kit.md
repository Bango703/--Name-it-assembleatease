## 3. Partner backlinks (the real needle-mover)

Earn a handful of genuine links from businesses whose customers need assembly. Track them below.

### Target types
Apartment & property managers · realtors · movers · furniture/retail stores · interior
designers · chambers of commerce · suppliers · any business you've completed real jobs for.

## 4. Reviews (already automated — your part is volume)

The platform auto-requests a review from every eligible completed customer and offers the
Google-review link. Your part:

- Keep completing jobs so the engine has customers to ask.
- Respond to every review (a short, genuine reply signals an active, trusted business).
- Never fabricate reviews or offer anything in exchange for a positive one.

---

## What this kit deliberately does not do

- It does not create fake listings, buy links, or auto-post reviews — those are the
  black-hat moves the plan rejects.
- It does not touch the website; execution here happens on third-party platforms with
  the business's own accounts.
