Good to know

How is it delivered?
By email, right after payment. The code goes to you or to the person you choose.
How is it used?
Enter the code when you book. The credit comes off the total, your payment card covers the rest, and nothing is charged until the work is done. Credit you don’t use stays on your gift card.
What is priority booking?
For 30 days after you buy a Move-In Pass, bookings made with it are offered to our pros before other bookings.
Does it expire?
Gift card credit never expires. With the Move-In Pass, the $49 you pay never expires; the $11 bonus and priority booking last 30 days from purchase.
Is it cash?
No. Credit cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for cash.
