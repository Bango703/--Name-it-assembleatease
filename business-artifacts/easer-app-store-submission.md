## Description

AssembleAtEase Easer is the work app for approved AssembleAtEase pros, the independent contractors who assemble furniture, mount TVs, and set up homes for customers.

Job offers
Get a notification the moment a job is offered to you. See the service, the area, the date and time, and what you will earn before you accept.

Your schedule
Every accepted job in one place, with the address, customer notes, and the items to assemble.

On the day
Tap On my way and Arrived so the customer knows when to expect you. Message the customer about the job without sharing your personal number. Add photos of the finished work from your camera.

Earnings and payouts
See estimated and final earnings for every job and the status of each payout.

The app is for approved AssembleAtEase Easers. To apply, visit assembleatease.com/become-an-easer.
