Book with your AI assistant

Ask Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant about furniture assembly, TV mounting or home setup in Texas. With the AssembleAtEase connector it sees our live prices, arrival windows and service area, and gives you a link to book.

Connector address

https://www.assembleatease.com/api/mcp

No account or sign-in is needed.

Add it to Claude

  1. Open Customize, then Connectors.
  2. Select Add, then Add custom connector.
  3. Name it AssembleAtEase, paste the address above and select Continue.
  4. For sign-in, choose No sign-in, then select Add.

Every Claude plan can add a connector this way. On a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner of the workspace adds it.

Add it to ChatGPT

In ChatGPT accounts that can add custom apps, create a new app with the address above and no authentication. Turn the app on in a chat to use it.

What your assistant can look up

  • Services and prices. Every service, its starting price, popular items and room bundles.
  • A job search. Items that match what you need done, with their fixed prices.
  • A price for your job. Items, any multi-service discount, the service-call fee, Texas sales tax and the total.
  • Arrival windows. The dates and two-hour windows you can choose when you book.
  • Where we work. The Texas ZIP codes where a local pro is matched automatically.
  • Booking terms. When your card is charged, cancellation fees, custom quotes and how to reach us.

Booking

The connector does not book or take payment. Your assistant gives you a link to our booking page with your items already in the cart. You choose the time, enter your address and confirm there, and the total is shown again before you book.

What the connector receives

Only what your assistant sends to look something up: search words, item names and quantities, or a date. It does not receive your conversation, name, phone number, email or address. We count lookups by type and by assistant, without what was asked. Our Privacy Notice covers everything else.
