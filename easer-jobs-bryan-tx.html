Questions from pros

Is this a job or independent contractor work?

Independent-contractor work. You set your own schedule, use your own tools, and are paid per completed job. You sign an independent-contractor agreement during onboarding.

How much do I earn?

You keep 70% of each job’s price before tax, plus 100% of tips. A TV mount, 56" to 65" at $154 pays you $107.80. Earnings depend on how many jobs you take; there are no guaranteed hours.

How and when do I get paid?

After the job is completed and confirmed, your earnings go to your bank through Stripe once your payout setup is done. Instant payout is available when your bank supports it.

Is there an application fee?

No application fee while your area is still adding Easers. Any fee for your area is shown before you apply.

Do I need insurance?

You keep any insurance the law requires for your work, and car insurance if you drive to jobs. General liability insurance is strongly recommended. AssembleAtEase does not provide insurance for contractors.

Do I bring parts or mounts?

No. You bring your own tools. Customers supply their own furniture, mounts and hardware.

Is there an app?

Yes. On iPhone, work your jobs in the Easer by AssembleAtEase app. On Android or a computer, use the Easer dashboard on the website.

How many jobs will I get in Bryan?

We accept customer bookings across Bryan and confirm a local pro for each one, so the opportunity is real, but Bryan is a newer market and volume is still growing. Getting approved now puts you in position to accept jobs as demand grows. Volume is demand-driven with no guaranteed hours.
